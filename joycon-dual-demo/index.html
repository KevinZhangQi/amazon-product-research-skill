Dual Joy-Con Platformer Demo

A browser game scaffold specifically shaped for two separate Joy-Con controllers: left Joy-Con drives Player 1, right Joy-Con drives Player 2.

How it works

  1. Click Connect Joy-Cons.
  2. Select one left Joy-Con and one right Joy-Con.
  3. The page pairs them to Player 1 and Player 2.
  4. Movement uses the bound Joy-Con gamepad input.

Best target: desktop Chrome or Edge. WebHID is used for Joy-Con discovery and side assignment.

Connection status

Controls

Left Joy-Con → Player 1

Stick or D-pad to move, lower face button to jump.

Right Joy-Con → Player 2

Stick or D-pad to move, lower face button to jump.

Keyboard fallback: A/D/W for Player 1, ←/→/↑ for Player 2.
